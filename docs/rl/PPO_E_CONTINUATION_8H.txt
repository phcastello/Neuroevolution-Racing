DIAGNOSIS AND SOURCE

Only E200/Interlagos, E400/Interlagos and E200/Silverstone were traced under
the historical implementation. All three reproduced the historical progress
values exactly, ending after 10,800 deterministic CPU decisions. CSV traces
contain every decision: position, signed speed, actions, local/global projection,
segment, signed accumulated progress, best progress, remaining distance, movement,
projection delta, rejected flag and terminal condition.

The policies continued moving forward around the track, without reverse motion.
Local and global projections agreed. On sharp corners, nearest-point projection
switched between immediate adjacent polyline segments. Its arclength jumped
farther than the physical displacement, triggering the old generic discontinuity
guard and erasing accumulated progress on every lap.

Example: E400/Interlagos, decision 1969, segments 323 -> 324:
  movement=2.3573954; projected delta=15.454102; accumulated progress=9828.138 -> 0.

The RL-only correction recognizes connected immediate-neighbor transitions,
including the track seam. A stored physical position rejects relocation. Signed
progress, reverse debt and the high-water reward prevent oscillation farming.
Non-adjacent projection jumps retain the existing discontinuity guard. The full
lap criterion, start reference, track geometry, GA, shared physics, observation
semantics, reward formula and reward coefficients were not changed.

Environment implementation identifier: rl-progress-connected-adjacent-v2

TIME CONTRACT

One Stopwatch/QPC origin starts at script entry, before manifest reads or setup.
The small prepared .NET Framework supervisor DLL is verified against its SHA-256
and auditable C# source. It is loaded without launching a bootstrap compiler.

Cooperative cutoff: 27,000 seconds (7h30) from script entry. Build and setup
time are deducted before launching the trainer, plus five seconds for process
handoff. The Rust monotonic clock also includes config/GPU/scene initialization.
Before each rollout and evaluation, the trainer checks conservative work margins:
  rollout floor 60 s; five-track CPU evaluation floor 300 s; final save floor 60 s.
  Observed durations can only increase each bound: max(previous, 3*duration+30).
The trainer stops starting work when the next unit plus the final-save reserve
does not fit. It saves a valid final checkpoint at a complete rollout boundary,
even away from a multiple of 100. stop_reason is wall_time_budget or rollout_limit.
A normal budget stop returns success. It does not try to finish 4000 afterwards.

The last 30 minutes are reserved for finalization/reporting or unexpected delay.
The outer Windows Job Object starts its worker suspended, assigns it before it
runs, and owns all descendants, including build tools, trainer and report.
A timer independent of the PowerShell wait loop terminates this job exceptionally
at 28,790 seconds (7h59m50), leaving 10 seconds to record supervision.json before
the eight-hour maximum. Closing the supervisor also closes/kills its owned job.
No global process search, name-based termination, service or machine shutdown.
The C# watchdog was exercised with a synthetic stuck parent/child tree while
a separate synthetic job remained alive.

PERSISTENCE, EVALUATION AND SELECTION

Every accumulated multiple of 100: checkpoint, losses and one deterministic CPU
attempt on each training track, with separate frozen networks/scenes/RNGs.
The source is evaluated once before any new training, using the actual compiled
runtime. That score participates in best selection. Ties keep the older rollout.
No best-policy rollback occurs during learning. Every periodic checkpoint stays.
Collection/update timings exclude persistence/evaluation timings.
Full experience dumps are disabled with --no-save-rollouts.

The final checkpoint is evaluated only if the conservative evaluation/save
margin remains. Otherwise it is explicitly unevaluated and cannot displace the
best measured checkpoint. The report distinguishes source, best and latest.

The existing save routine serializes into a new temporary file, flushes/syncs it,
then publishes by same-directory rename. Existing checkpoint names are refused.
Only published checkpoints enter checkpoint_index.csv. A failed new write cannot
replace an older checkpoint. Temporary files are never resume candidates.

RECOVERY INTO ANOTHER SESSION

Use the exact latest_checkpoint from the completed comparison manifest, or a
complete checkpoint_index.csv record after exceptional interruption. Never use
a temporary file or choose a directory by date. The resume loader validates the
entire checkpoint. A new session has its own complete eight-hour budget and
preserves the previous branch:

  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/run_ppo_e_continuation_8h.ps1 -ResumeCheckpoint "<exact intact checkpoint path>"

The cap remains 4000 accumulated rollouts; a checkpoint already at 4000 does not
authorize additional rollouts. The script preserves a new source copy and runs
its fresh baseline evaluation under the verified implementation. An exhausted
budget before training records a planned wall_time_budget stop without training.
